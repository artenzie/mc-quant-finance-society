# SESSION B2 — Open Society, Week 2

**Date:** Thursday 24 September 2026
**Duration:** 1 hour (17:00–18:00)
**Attendance:** 19 — Artem Makarov, Kevin Wang, Kate Stepanova, Safienaaz Chugtai, Aarin Taurani, Victor Johnson, Archie Davies, Cosmo Simpson, Henry Fletcher, Joe Stevens, Seb Selby, Vico Chiefa, Will Jordan, Yadur Mohindroo, Zain Halim, Ollie Raphael, Lucas Ellis, Victory Montgomery, Roscoe Chilton

**Summary:** Figgie again, bigger turnout

**What happened:**
This was aimed at catching people who missed session one and playing Figgie again, partly to get a read on who's likely to keep coming. Turnout more than doubled. Wasim couldn't make it (basketball practice), so the start was slower without him, but it picked up fast once trading began. We ran three tables of around five, with some early leavers and informal mid-session substitutions — people effectively benching themselves in and out. Compared to the all-in ending of session one, this week's strategies were much more varied — some tables playing cautiously until the last moment, others gambling early.

Same sign-up sheet as week one, with the Instagram option — the group chat is now live with 20+ members and still growing.

**Notable moments:** People who'd already played, or had looked at the rules beforehand, clearly influenced their tables — the goal suit was correctly worked out by the end of almost every round, a sharp contrast to session one where it was almost never guessed.

**Outcome:** Will Jordan won — his first time playing, against a table of more experienced players, winning 3 of 4 rounds. Got chocolate, was very happy.

**Assessment:** Everyone enjoyed it, and a lot of people thanked me afterward.
