# SESSION B1 — Open Society, Week 1

**Date:** Thursday 17 September 2026
**Duration:** 1 hour (17:00–18:00, during studies)
**Attendance:** 8 — Artem Makarov, Wasim Nazirzadeh, Henry Fletcher, Aarin Taurani, Harrison Moore, Hugh Sherwin, Joe Stevens, Seb Selby

**Summary:** Figgie launch

**What happened:**
I introduced the club, then we went straight into Figgie across two tables. I explained the rules; the room was a bit confused at first and expecting a different format, so it took a moment to get going. I borrowed $350 in poker chips per player from Hugh Sherwin, and built the decks beforehand from four packs of cards into labelled envelopes. We played five rounds, four minutes each, switching tables after rounds 2 and 4. I rang a bell at the start of each round like the NYSE opening bell, and called "cards down" at the end of trading. After round 3 I introduced the core strategy tip — work out the 12-card suit first, since the goal suit shares its colour, and watch how people are behaving. At the end of the final round, everyone decided to go all-in rather than the usual buy-in, so each table effectively played for one winner.

I also took names on a sign-up sheet, with an option to be added to an Instagram group chat.

**Notable moments:** Open-outcry trading got properly chaotic and funny — people shouting mismatched trades (e.g. "a spade for ten" and then only handing over five). Energy built through the rounds; by the end nobody wanted to leave.

**Outcome:** Two prizes — Henry Fletcher won with the most games, and Seb Selby won with the most chips by the end (though the final-round all-in and some people leaving early make that less exact than it sounds). Both got chocolate to share with the room and keep some for themselves. Nobody properly worked out the goal suit — mostly luck, though a few people were clearly improving by the later rounds.

**Assessment:** A strong first session — I got a lot of people hooked.
